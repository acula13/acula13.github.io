About

Michael Kenealy

Building practical data analytics skills through
Northeastern University's MSDAE program.

I'm Michael Kenealy, a graduate student in Northeastern University's online Master of Science in Data Analytics Engineering (College of Engineering). This site highlights selected coursework and projects across data mining, computation and visualization, database design, and case-study machine learning.

My work emphasizes end-to-end workflows: framing a real problem, acquiring and cleaning data, exploratory analysis, modeling, and communicating results with clear visuals and documentation.

Coursework featured here

  • DADS 6400 — Data Mining for Engineering Applications (clustering, time-series features, text networks)
  • DADS 6600 — Computation and Visualization for Analytics
  • DADS 6700 — Data Management for Analytics (SQL, ER modeling, MySQL project)
  • DADS 7275 — Data Mining Case Study (SpaceX Falcon 9 landing prediction)

Tools I use

Python (pandas, scikit-learn, XGBoost, SHAP, Matplotlib/Seaborn), SQL / MySQL, Jupyter notebooks, Git & GitHub, and web fundamentals for presenting results.
