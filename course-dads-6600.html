DADS 6600 · Computation & Visualization for Analytics

Data Visualization
& Exploration Labs

Using visualization for data exploration, knowledge discovery,
and decision-making with Python.

Course focus

IE / DADS 6600 covers principles of visualization and practical tools for exploring engineering, operations, and related datasets. Coursework spans static and interactive charts with attention to color encoding, layout, annotation, and data storytelling.

Lab work completed

  • Data cleaning & missing values — diabetes, housing, and multi-source tables
  • Exploratory analysis — athlete events, Superstore, Netflix titles, wine quality, world happiness
  • Matplotlib / Seaborn charts — line, bar, histogram, scatter, heatmaps, and complex multi-panel layouts
  • Interactive visualization — techniques for drill-down and comparison
  • Time series & markets — Facebook stock price labs and renewable energy consumption by country

Outcomes

Ability to explore unfamiliar datasets quickly, choose chart types that match analytical questions, critique visual design, and implement reproducible notebooks that communicate insights clearly.
